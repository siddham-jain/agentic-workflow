# Financial Formulas

## Compound Interest
Formula: A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt)
Where: A = final amount, P = principal, r = annual rate, n = compounding periods per year, t = time in years
Example: $10,000 at 5% compounded annually for 3 years = $11,576.25

## Current Yield
Formula: Current Yield = (Annual Coupon Payment / Current Market Price) × 100
Where: Annual Coupon = Face Value × Coupon Rate
Example: Bond with $1,000 face value, 5% coupon, trading at $980
Annual Coupon = $1,000 × 0.05 = $50
Current Yield = ($50 / $980) × 100 = 5.10%

## P/E Ratio
Formula: P/E Ratio = Stock Price / Earnings Per Share (EPS)
Where: EPS = Net Income / Outstanding Shares
Example: Stock price $150, EPS $10 → P/E = 15

## Return on Investment (ROI)
Formula: ROI = ((Final Value - Initial Value) / Initial Value) × 100

## Net Present Value (NPV)
Formula: NPV = Σ(Ct / (1 + r)^t) - C0
